## Your repo, as a world

Guildhall turns a code repository into a living low-poly island. Every folder becomes a village, the largest package becomes the capital, roads join the places, and the land grows from the repository's whole history, season by season.

And it is live. Connect Claude Code and the world reacts: heroes walk to the code they read, scaffolding rises where they edit, a storm rolls in when tests fail, a dragon shows up on a failing streak, and fireworks light the sky when a quest ships clean.

## What you can do

- **Grow any repo:** React, VS Code, opencode and more have full chronicles; watch them grow from the first commit.
- **Watch agents work:** a local hub turns Claude Code hook events into heroes, quests and weather.
- **Share your island:** the site films your island and draws a Chronicle card to download or share.
- **Explore the Studio:** the workshop the world is built with, open to everyone, nothing saved.

Read how it was built in the **Building Guildhall** series, starting with [Give the AI Eyes: The Studio That Saved Guildhall](/experiments/guildhall-studio-give-ai-eyes).
